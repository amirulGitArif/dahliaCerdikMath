Privacy Policy

Anak Cerdik · Last updated: October 15, 2026

Anak Cerdik is a preschool learning app with maths, money, and time activities. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

Anak Cerdik does not ask you to create an account and does not collect or send personal information to us. The app does not use advertising or analytics services.

Information stored on your device

If you use Quiz mode, the app saves quiz scores, answers, and completion dates locally on your device so you can view your quiz history. This information is not transmitted to us. You can remove it at any time from Settings by choosing “Reset data.”

Children’s privacy

Anak Cerdik is designed for preschool learning. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s features or data practices change. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer using the contact information on the app’s store listing.
